# ELURA Pitch Deck — final 16:9 image deck

## Direction

- Audience: pre-seed / seed investors
- Language: English
- Format: one 16:9 PNG per slide
- Visual system: Toss-like clarity and generous whitespace, Airbnb-like warmth and human spaces
- Palette: off-white, near-black, electric blue `#1457FF`, pale blue gradients, warm beige room photography
- Type: large geometric sans serif, short lines, minimal body copy
- Truth standard: pre-revenue; audience and infrastructure signals are not presented as paid traction

## Storyline

### 01 — Cover

Headline: **Reveal yourself through your space.**

Subhead: Personalized art designed around you and your space.

### 02 — Problem

Headline: **A home can be fully furnished—and still feel unfinished.**

Support: People know what they love, but struggle to translate it into a space that feels like them.

### 03 — Why it stays unfinished

Headline: **The more personal the decision, the harder it is to make with confidence.**

Flow: I know what I love → What belongs here? → Will it feel like me? → Maybe later.

Bottom line: **Not a daily purchase. A daily reminder.**

### 04 — Insight

Headline: **The market solved inspiration. It never solved translation.**

Visual: inspiration abundance → visible translation gap → actual room.

Bottom line: The missing layer is not more choice. It is confident translation.

### 05 — Why now

Headline: **Home personalization can now begin with the person—not the catalog.**

Three shifts:

1. Taste is visible — 631M monthly Pinterest users; 80B+ monthly searches.
2. Space is understandable — multimodal systems can interpret references and real rooms together.
3. One-of-one is fulfillable — on-demand production works without traditional inventory.

Bottom line: Personalization is moving from choosing among products to creating what belongs.

### 06 — Solution

Headline: **ELURA translates who you are into how your space feels.**

Visual: YOU → ELURA → YOUR SPACE.

Bottom line: The translation layer between people and their spaces.

### 07 — Core experience

Headline: **It starts with understanding you.**

Inputs: Room · Places · Music · Memories · Colors.

Output: three curated art directions shown in the actual room.

Bottom line: **Not infinite generation. Curated confidence.**

### 08 — Result

Headline: **Personal to you. Natural in your space.**

Before / after plus four deliverables: Curated artwork · Room preview · Size & frame guide · Print or delivery.

Bottom line: A home that reflects the person living in it.

### 09 — Target customer

Headline: **Women creating their first truly personal home.**

Profile: 27–39 · Urban U.S., Canada, U.K. · Apartment / condo / flat · Renter or first-time owner.

Triggers: Moved · Living together · First home · Room refresh.

Bottom line: The home is almost finished. It just does not feel like them yet.

### 10 — Market

Headline: **A large category. A focused starting point.**

- **$55.5B** global wall-decor market, 2024
- **8.8% CAGR** through 2030
- **$92.0B** projected global wall-decor market, 2030
- **6–9M** English-speaking urban home personalizers in the initial beachhead, internal estimate
- **25K–100K** annual paying customers targeted in 3–5 years

Bottom line: Art is our entry point into home personalization.

### 11 — Business model

Headline: **Start with guidance. Grow into the room.**

- **$19** Room Art Direction Pack — direction, room preview, print & frame guide
- **$49** Founder-led Direction — three curated directions and personal PDF
- **$150–250** Personalized Art — printed / framed physical product, planned core commerce

Footnote: Pre-revenue. Pricing and physical margins will be validated with the first 30 paid orders.

### 12 — Validation

Headline: **We built the learning loop. Paid conversion is next.**

- **17.6K** viewers in the latest measured 7-day window
- **49** archived audience / content reports
- **166** generated content batches in the workspace
- **Live prototype** for inquiry, direction generation, art generation, login and admin monitoring

Next proof: First 30 paid orders; close checkout → room upload → delivery → satisfaction measurement.

Footnote: Audience signals as of July 5, 2026. They are not revenue claims.

### 13 — Go to market

Headline: **Every personalized space brings in the next customer.**

Loop: TikTok discovery → Instagram trust → Room application → Paid transformation → Customer story → repeat.

Focus: Urban women 27–39 · U.S., Canada, U.K. · organic transformations + micro-creators first.

Bottom line: Content is not just marketing. It is an output of the product.

### 14 — Competition

Headline: **The market forces a trade-off. ELURA removes it.**

Map axes: Generic → Deeply personal; Self-directed → Guided and space-aware.

Groups: Inspiration / catalogs · DIY AI tools · Custom artists / interior designers · ELURA.

Bottom line: Designer-level personalization. Product-level accessibility.

### 15 — Defensibility

Headline: **The image is easy to copy. The translation system gets stronger with every home.**

Formula: Identity clues + Actual room + Real outcomes → ELURA Translation System.

Layers: Translation data · Curated system · Brand trust · End-to-end operations.

Bottom line: Not a better image generator. A better system for turning identity into space.

### 16 — Roadmap

Headline: **From concierge proof to a compounding product.**

- Now — prototype + content learning loop
- Next 6 months — first 30 paid orders; checkout, upload and delivery closed
- Then — 100 paid orders; structured identity × room × outcome data
- Scale — automate direction, fulfillment and repeat / gift products

Bottom line: Prove willingness to pay first. Automate what customers repeatedly value.

### 17 — Closing

Headline: **The future of home personalization starts with the person.**

Brand line: ELURA · Reveal yourself through your space.
